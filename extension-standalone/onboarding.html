Welcome to PechPech 👋

An extension that records your online meetings and hands you ready-made meeting minutes without any note-taking. This version needs no local server — everything runs directly from your browser.

🎙
Smart meeting recording
Simultaneously records and mixes browser tab audio (Google Meet, Teams, Zoom Web) and your microphone. No extra software needed.
💾
Local browser storage
Recordings are stored directly in your browser storage (IndexedDB) — no files on disk and no intermediary server.
📝
Speech-to-text & meeting minutes
Audio is sent directly to an STT service (OpenAI Whisper or Gemini), then a language model writes the summary, decisions, and action items.

Initial setup

You just need an API key for STT and a key for the LLM — no server to install or run.

1
Get an API key
Get a free/paid API key from platform.openai.com or aistudio.google.com.
2
Open the extension settings
Click "Open extension settings" below, choose your STT and LLM service, and enter the API key.
3
Save
Click "Save settings". You're all set — move to the next step to check the connection.

Check connection

Let's make sure at least one STT key and one LLM key are set.

Checking…
Haven't entered a key yet?

then click "Recheck".

You're all set!

PechPech is installed and waiting for your first meeting.

🎙 On your next meeting, open the Google Meet or Teams tab, click the PechPech icon in the Chrome toolbar, and click Start Recording.
⏹ At the end of the meeting, click Stop & Save. The audio file is stored right here in this browser.
📄 Click Process and wait a few minutes. The final minutes (summary + decisions + action items) appear in the extension popup.
✏️ If the transcript needs correcting, click Correct Transcript to have the LLM review it.
